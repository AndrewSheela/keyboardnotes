How to connect
1. Cable: USB-C to USB-B into USB TO HOST (NOT TO DEVICE). I455: TO HOST is square port. If phone shows Transfer files, you are in TO DEVICE.
1b. I455: FUNCTION > MIDI > Keyboard Out=ON, Style Out=OFF, Song Out=OFF.
2. Turn ON keyboard.
3. Open https://jgeneration.org/chord-reader.html in Chrome on Android (top-level page, NOT inside Meta AI / NOT in an iframe). Tap Connect MIDI > Allow.
iPhone Safari does NOT support Web MIDI.
